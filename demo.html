What you just watched

No prompts. No clicking. Just a finished job.

Behind every line of that transcript is a real agent doing real work — picking the model, reading your systems, taking the action, writing the result back. Here's what makes it different from a chatbot.

It acts

Writes to your real systems

The agent isn't telling you what to do — it's logging into your EMR, CRM, ERP or phone stack and doing it. With permissions, audit trails and rollback on every action.

It decides

Knows when to escalate

Every workflow has rules for when a human owns the next step. The agent hands off with full context — transcript, attempted actions, suggested fix — so the human doesn't start cold.

It bills

Charges per finished outcome

Every line you saw is logged against the contract SLA. Outcomes that ship are billed. Failed attempts and escalations are not. The meter mirrors the work.
