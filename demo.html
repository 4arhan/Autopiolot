What you just watched

Three phases. Ten seconds. One finished job.

Every Autopilot Labs agent runs the same loop: read the input, check it against your system of record, ship the outcome where it belongs. Runs on our in-house AI cloud, or inside your VPC, data centre or air-gapped enclave — built for large enterprise and federal/government workloads.

Phase 01 · Parse

Read the input, field by field

The agent opens the incoming document — a fax, an invoice, a litigation exhibit, a CSV — and extracts structured fields: names, dates, amounts, references. OCR, vision and table extraction are built in; nothing for you to configure.

Plain English: a fast, careful reader that never mistypes
Phase 02 · Validate

Cross-check the system of record

It doesn't guess. Every extracted field is checked against your source of truth — the EMR, the ledger, the matter file. The agent scores its own confidence, and anything below the threshold is routed to a human before it ships.

Plain English: if it isn't sure, a human sees it first
Phase 03 · Ship

Post the outcome where it belongs

The finished job lands in the system your team already uses — Epic, NetSuite, Relativity — with every action logged and timestamped. You're billed only when the outcome passes its SLA. Miss the SLA, you don't pay.

Plain English: the work just appears, done and audited
About the demo

Common questions, straight answers.

Is this the real AI, or a recording?

The log is scripted so the full story fits in ten seconds, but the phases, fields and timings mirror production. Book an audit and we'll give you a read-only seat on the real ops console.

Do you see my data?

No. Everything on this page runs locally in your browser. Nothing is sent, logged or stored — we never know you were here unless you book.

Can the AI be wrong?

Yes — and it's built to know when. Every agent scores its own confidence; anything below the threshold goes to a human reviewer before it ships. You never pay for an outcome that failed SLA.

How accurate are the cost figures?

They match the 90-day rolling medians from our production console — healthcare intake runs a touch faster, large legal exhibits a touch slower.

My workflow isn't shown here.

These three cover our most common agent shapes. If yours is different — claims triage, lease abstraction, return disputes — we'll build a bespoke demo on the audit call, with your own sample data.

How soon could this run for me?

Median audit-to-production is 14 days after contract. From first call to a live agent, you're looking at about a month — no new hires, no new SaaS.
