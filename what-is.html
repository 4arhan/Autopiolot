What's inside one agent.

Four parts, always. Different model, different prompts, different integrations per workflow — but the shape is the same. We're not selling you LLM access; we're selling you a unit of finished work.

01

A model picked for the job

GPT-5, Claude Opus 4.7, Gemini, Llama, a fine-tuned 8B — whichever wins on accuracy + cost for this specific workflow. We re-evaluate quarterly.

02

A workflow brain

The rules, scripts, escalation paths, allowed actions. Pulled from your SOPs, not invented by us. You sign off before we ship.

03

Live integrations

Read/write into your CRM, EMR, helpdesk, phone, calendar. OAuth, SCIM, webhooks — all wired. The agent isn't watching from outside; it's inside the system doing the work.

04

An outcome meter

Every action is logged, scored, and timestamped against the contractually-defined outcome. The invoice mirrors the meter — passes only.

The questions every buyer asks.

Why not just use ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini directly?

You can. For ad-hoc queries, that's the right tool. But "ad-hoc" stops scaling the moment a real workflow has a deadline, a written outcome, or audit liability. A vertical agent wraps the LLM in scripts, integrations, escalation rules, and an SLA — so the same underlying model becomes a service you can put in a contract, not a tool one person fiddles with.

How is this different from a chatbot or a copilot?

A chatbot answers questions in a window — the human still does the work. A copilot suggests inside an app — the human still hits "accept." A vertical agent closes the loop: it takes the call, writes the file, dispatches the truck, books the appointment, posts the entry. The outcome shows up in your system without a human in the middle.

What does "billed per outcome" actually mean?

We define the outcome in writing — "one resolved ticket," "one booked inspection," "one reconciled invoice" — agree the unit price, and then the meter only ticks when the agent ships a clean pass. Failed attempts, retries, escalations to a human: free. You see the per-outcome ledger live, the same view our ops team uses.

How long from "we should try this" to live in production?

Average 4 to 8 weeks from a one-page brief to a live agent shipping outcomes against your SLA. Faster on workflows we've shipped before; slower on novel verticals that need new integrations. Either way: we don't bill until the meter is live.

What if our use case isn't on your site?

It usually isn't. Every engagement is custom-built — the workflows we list are samples of what we've shipped, not the menu. Send us a paragraph describing what your team does on repeat; we'll come back with a quote in a week.

Who owns the agent — us or you?

You own the workflow definition, the data, the integrations, and the SOPs. We own the operational responsibility — model selection, tuning, monitoring, on-call. If you ever want to take it in-house, the brief and the playbook come with you. We don't trap you in a lock-in.
