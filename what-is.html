FAQ

The questions every buyer asks.

Why not just use ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini directly?

For ad-hoc queries, you should — that's the right tool. But ad-hoc stops scaling the moment a workflow has a deadline, a written outcome or audit liability. A vertical agent wraps the model in scripts, integrations, escalation rules and an SLA, so the same underlying technology becomes a service you can put in a contract — not a tool one person fiddles with.

How is this different from a chatbot or a copilot?

A chatbot answers questions in a window — the human still does the work. A copilot suggests inside an app — the human still hits "accept." A vertical agent closes the loop: it takes the call, writes the file, dispatches the truck, books the appointment, posts the entry. The outcome lands in your system without a human in the middle.

What does "billed per outcome" actually mean?

We define the outcome in writing — "one resolved ticket," "one booked inspection," "one reconciled invoice" — agree the unit price, and the meter only ticks when the agent ships a clean pass. Failed attempts, retries and escalations to a human are free. You see the per-outcome ledger live, the same view our ops team uses.

How long from "we should try this" to live in production?

On average 4 to 8 weeks from a one-page brief to a live agent shipping outcomes against your SLA — faster on workflows we've shipped before, slower on novel verticals that need new integrations. Either way, we don't bill until the meter is live.

What if our use case isn't on your site?

It usually isn't. Every engagement is custom-built — the workflows we list are samples of what we've shipped, not the menu. Send us a paragraph describing what your team does on repeat and we'll come back with a quote in a week.

Who owns the agent — us or you?

You own the workflow definition, the data, the integrations and the SOPs. We own the operational responsibility — model selection, tuning, monitoring, on-call. If you ever want to take it in-house, the brief and the playbook come with you. No lock-in.
