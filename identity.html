§ Section 01 01 Logomark
Mark

A square, a cobalt cut.

The Autopilot Labs mark is a solid ink square with a single cobalt cut at the bottom-right. It reads as a checkbox — a job, marked done. Built on a 24-unit grid. Used at five canonical sizes.

Clearspace § 01.f

Give the mark one square of air on every side.

The minimum clearspace around the logomark is equal to the width of the cobalt cut — one quarter of the mark's edge. No type, image, or edge may enter this zone. Treat it as an absolute boundary.

Expressed as clearspace = mark-size × 0.25. At 96px, clearspace is 24px. At 256px, clearspace is 64px.

§ Section 03 03 Typography
Type

Geist & JetBrains Mono.

Two typefaces. Geist across the display and body range. JetBrains Mono for labels, coordinates, data. No serif. No italic — emphasis is cobalt weight-500, never slanted.

§ 03.a · body

Autopilot Labs builds vertical AI agents for thirteen industries — from patient intake to reconciliation to lease abstraction. You pay when the work ships, not for seats or tokens. Every engagement starts with a workflow audit, not a product demo. Our body text sits at sixteen pixels, line-height one point six-five, Geist Regular, ink-2. Paragraphs run no wider than sixty-eight characters for comfortable scanning. When a phrase needs weight, we shift it to cobalt-medium — never italic, never uppercase, never bold.

§ Section 06 06 Motion
Motion

Linear only. No bounce.

All motion uses linear timing. Reveal is 600ms. Hover is 250ms. Scroll progress tracks 1:1. Motion is structural, not decorative — it tells you where you are, not how clever we are.

§ Section 07 07 Voice
Voice

Plain, exact, accountable.

We write like we ship: precisely, without hedging. Six lexical rules. Struck-through words are blacklisted — use the cobalt replacement.

§ 07.a
We say Seats
We don't say Licences
"Seats" keeps the conversation about humans. "Licences" is procurement-speak.
§ 07.b
We say Outcome
We don't say Deliverable
We bill per outcome. Deliverables are what agencies invoice when work isn't done.
§ 07.c
We say Ship
We don't say Launch
"Ship" is verb-first. "Launch" is marketing theatre.
§ 07.d
We say Agent
We don't say Copilot
Agents finish jobs. Copilots sit shotgun and hedge.
§ 07.e
We say Workflow
We don't say Solution
A workflow has steps, owners, and SLAs. A "solution" is a slide.
§ 07.f
We say Audit
We don't say Discovery
Audits end in a number — hours saved, errors cut. Discovery ends in a deck.
