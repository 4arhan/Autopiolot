    What an AI agent can do today

    If your team does it every day, an AI agent can do it for you.

    Almost any repetitive task — the kind a person learns in a week and then does for years — can now be handled by an AI agent. Below are 12 of the most common jobs we're automating in 2026, in plain English. None of these are demos. All of them are running in production today.

    Email · Tier-1

    Reading and replying to email

    The agent reads every email that lands in your inbox, drafts the reply, sends the easy ones itself, and only flags the tricky ones to a human — with a one-line summary attached.

    Calendar · scheduling

    Booking and rebooking meetings

    It handles the back-and-forth — "are you free Tuesday?" — across time zones, finds a slot that works, sends the invite, and reschedules when someone cancels.

    Finance · AP

    Processing supplier invoices

    It opens the PDF, reads the line items, matches the invoice to the purchase order, codes it to the right account, and queues it for payment. No more chasing finance.

    Finance · expenses

    Filing expense reports

    Photo of the receipt goes to the agent. It reads the receipt, applies your policy, fills the form, attaches the proof, and submits it to the right approver. Done.

    Support · phone & chat

    Answering customer questions

    It reads your help docs and past tickets, answers questions on chat and phone the same way your best agent would, and only escalates the cases that genuinely need a human.

    Reputation · reviews

    Replying to online reviews

    Every new Google, TripAdvisor or App Store review gets a reply in your brand tone, within minutes — including the bad ones, with an apology and a follow-up to a manager.

    Hiring · screening

    Screening CVs and booking interviews

    It reads every CV, scores against the role, calls the shortlist for a 15-minute screen, books the keepers into the hiring manager's diary, and writes a one-page summary into your ATS.

    Sales · qualifying

    Qualifying inbound leads

    When a new lead comes in, the agent emails or calls within minutes, asks the qualifying questions, and only books a real meeting with your sales team if the lead is a fit.

    Marketing · content ops

    Writing and posting content

    From a one-line brief, the agent drafts the blog, the LinkedIn post and the email, formats them for each channel, schedules them, and reports on what worked the next week.

    Meetings · notes

    Taking meeting notes and follow-ups

    The agent joins your Zoom, writes the notes, lists the actions and who owns them, and emails the recap to everyone within five minutes of the meeting ending.

    Ops · returns

    Handling returns and refunds

    Customer wants a refund — the agent checks the policy, processes the refund, generates the return label, updates the order, and sends the customer the email. All without a ticket.

    Ops · stock

    Reordering stock before it runs out

    It watches your inventory levels and sales pace, predicts when each SKU will run out, raises the purchase order with the supplier, and emails the warehouse to expect it.

    Finance · payroll

    Running weekly payroll

    It collects timesheets, applies the awards, calculates super and tax, reconciles against the bank, and queues the pay run for one-click approval. The exceptions land on a single page.

    Ops · vendor onboarding

    Onboarding a new supplier or contractor

    The agent runs the KYC and ABN checks, requests insurance certificates, files the W-9 / W-8, sets up the bank record, and pings the buyer when the vendor is ready to transact.

    Legal · contracts

    Reviewing & redlining contracts

    Drop in an NDA, MSA or vendor agreement — the agent reads it against your playbook, flags risky clauses with rationale, drafts the redline, and escalates only the genuinely novel terms.

    IT · helpdesk

    Handling Tier-1 IT tickets

    Password resets, VPN issues, software access, mailbox quotas — the agent diagnoses, fixes via your IdP and MDM, closes the ticket, and only paged a human when the fix needs hands.

    Sales · outbound

    Drafting personalised outbound

    From an ICP definition, the agent researches every prospect, drafts a personalised email or LinkedIn message in your voice, sends the cadence, and only puts qualified replies on a human's calendar.

    Customer success · onboarding

    Walking new customers through setup

    It greets every new sign-up, runs a guided setup in chat, checks they hit "first value" within day one, and flags the accounts at risk of churn before the human CSM even logs in.

    Compliance · audit

    Pulling evidence for the audit

    When the SOC 2 / ISO / internal audit arrives, the agent gathers the evidence — access logs, change tickets, training records — maps it to controls, and assembles the auditor's binder in minutes, not weeks.

    Marketing · social

    Replying to social media and DMs

    Every comment, mention and DM across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and X gets a brand-voice reply within minutes — and escalates anyone with a real intent to buy, complain or PR-risk.

    Why we exist

    Three things every other AI agency gets wrong.

    Most AI agencies sell a chatbot and a subscription. We sell finished work, measured in units your CFO already tracks.

    Workflow-first

    We audit before we build.

    Every engagement starts with a two-week workflow audit. We map the manual path end-to-end, find the bottlenecks, and price only the jobs we can confidently automate. No audit, no build.

    Outcome pricing

    You pay per completed task.

    Tokens are our cost, not yours. Seats are a rounding error. You pay a rate per finished unit — a triaged claim, a tagged lease, a resolved ticket — with an SLA in the contract. Miss it, you don't pay.

    Vertical depth

    Specialised. Custom-built where it counts.

    We don't do "AI for everything." Specialised industries plus a multilingual voice desk — and a custom vertical when you bring us one we haven't shipped. We already know what a SOAP note and an FNOL packet need.

    Autopilot Labs vs. the platforms

    Most AI vendors sell you a model. We ship the finished work.

    Off-the-shelf agent builders hand you a toolbox and a bill per token — the starting line, not the finish. We run the last mile: agents embedded in your workflows, audited live, billed per outcome that passes SLA. If the job doesn't ship, you don't pay.
