Four phases · one fortnight

How a workflow becomes an agent in production.

The audit is on us. We only quote when we find work we can confidently put under an SLA — and about a third of audits end with us walking away and handing you the diagnostic.

Phase 01 · Brief

Tell us the workflow — one page is enough.

Send a paragraph describing the repetitive work: who does it, how often, in which systems. If it's easier, we sit inside your operation for two days and write the brief for you. All we need at this stage is a named owner on your side who knows the workflow first-hand.

  • One-page brief — yours or written by us, free
  • A named workflow owner on your side
  • Agreement on which workflow to audit first
Days 0–2 · You receive: a scoped audit plan
Phase 02 · Audit & quote

We measure the work, then price the outcome.

A two-person team embeds with your operators for five working days — shadowing 3–5 people end-to-end, running a time-and-motion study, and mapping every system the work touches. From the shortlist, we quote one price per finished outcome, with an SLA and a 30-day kill-switch. If we can't ship confidently, we say so and walk.

  • 18–24 page audit report — you keep it either way, A$0
  • 3–5 agent candidates ranked by ROI
  • 6–8 page statement of work: per-outcome price, volume tiers, SLA & credit-back schedule, data-handling terms
Days 1–8 · You receive: audit report + quote
Phase 03 · Build & integrate

The agent runs beside your team before it replaces any work.

We build in about four working days — on your data, wired into your systems via OAuth, SSO and SCIM. The agent then runs in shadow mode: it produces every outcome, but a human still owns the final click. Only when it matches the human on 200 consecutive tasks do we flip it live, against go-live criteria agreed in writing. Runs on our in-house AI cloud, or inside your VPC, data centre or air-gapped enclave — built for large enterprise and federal/government workloads.

  • Agent built on your data, not a generic model
  • Side-by-side shadow dashboard, 48–72 hours
  • Go-live gate: 200 clean consecutive runs — shadow period is free
Days 9–12 · You receive: a running agent in shadow
Phase 04 · Ship & bill per outcome

Live. Measured. Paid only when it passes.

The agent goes live and the meter ticks per clean pass. Every outcome appears in your ops dashboard — timestamped, costed, audit-trailed — with Slack or Teams alerting. SLA misses credit back automatically on the next invoice; you never raise a ticket. Monthly outcome reviews, quarterly rate reviews, and a 30-day off-ramp where your weights and data leave with you.

  • Real-time ops dashboard · invoice based only on SLA-passing work
  • Automatic credit-back for misses
  • 30-day exit: weights, logs and exports handed over
Day 14+ · You receive: outcomes, monthly
The deal, plainly

What we need from you. What you get back.

What we need from you

  • A named owner who knows the workflow first-hand
  • Access to 3–5 operators for five days of shadowing
  • Sample data and read access to the systems involved
  • Legal review of a 6–8 page statement of work (~48h)
  • A yes or no at the end of the quote — that's it

What you get

  • An 18–24 page audit report you keep, free, either way
  • One per-outcome price with SLA and credit-back schedule in writing
  • A production agent integrated into your existing systems
  • A real-time ops dashboard with every outcome audit-trailed
  • A 30-day exit where weights, logs and data leave with you
The guarantee

Miss the SLA, it's free. No audit, no build.

These aren't marketing lines — they're clauses in every statement of work. If we ever violate one, the engagement terminates that day and every outcome billed in the breach period is refunded.

SLA miss = free

Misses refund automatically

No support ticket, no negotiation. Outcomes that miss the SLA credit back on the next invoice without you asking.

No audit, no build

We turn work down

If the audit shows the workflow isn't ripe — not enough volume, too much judgement, bad data — we say so and walk. Roughly a third of audits end this way, and you keep the diagnostic.

No seats · no tokens

You pay for finished work only

Inference cost is our problem, seats punish adoption. If our models get cheaper, the saving is passed on at the quarterly rate review, in writing.

Human final click

People approve the irreversible

For every regulated or high-blast-radius workflow, a human signs off before anything irreversible happens. We make that click fast — not absent.

Your data, your exit

Everything leaves with you

Weights trained on your corpus are yours. On exit, we hand over weights, logs and exports within 30 days — and purge our side.

Plain English

Contracts your ops team can read

If a clause needs a lawyer to translate, we rewrite it. Your ops team should be able to audit our SLA without legal on the call.
