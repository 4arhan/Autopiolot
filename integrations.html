§ 02 — Under the hood

Every integration ships with the same four things.

— 01
Scoped auth

Least-privilege OAuth scopes, per-agent. Credentials encrypted at rest with customer-held KMS keys.

— 02
Audit log

Every read, write, and webhook logged with agent ID, reason, and diff. Exportable to SIEM on a daily tap.

— 03
Kill switch

Revoke an AI agent's access to any system in one click. Revocation is irrevocable — no background re-auth.

— 04
Rate + retry

Native throttling honours every vendor's published limits. Idempotent retries on 429 / 5xx with exponential backoff.
