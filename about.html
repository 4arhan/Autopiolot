How we work · Four articles

The rules we
won't negotiate.

Article 01

Audit before build.

Every engagement begins with a free two-week workflow audit. If the math doesn't work, we say so and leave the diagnostic behind. No audit, no build — no exceptions.

Article 02

Outcomes are the only unit.

Seats are a rounding error. Tokens are our cost. You pay per finished unit with an SLA in the contract. Miss the SLA, the outcome is credited back automatically.

Article 03

Specialised. Or custom-built.

We turn down work outside our specialised practice areas — and custom-build a new vertical when yours isn't on the shelf. Depth beats breadth. We already know what a SOAP note is and why your LegalHold tag matters — no relearning on your retainer.

Article 04

A human signs every outcome.

Every SLA-gated workflow has a named reviewer on our side, not just a monitoring dashboard. An agent runs the work. A person is accountable for it.
