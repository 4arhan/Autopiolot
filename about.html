How we work

The rules we won't negotiate.

Four principles, in writing, on every statement of work. They keep your risk low and our incentives honest.

01 · Audit-first

Two weeks, free.

Every engagement begins with a free two-week workflow audit — observing, measuring, mapping. We only propose what we can confidently ship.

02 · No audit, no build

We'll say no.

If the math doesn't work, we say so and leave the diagnostic behind. No retainers for vapour, no exceptions.

03 · Paid on outcomes

Miss SLA, free.

Seats are a rounding error and tokens are our cost. You pay per finished unit against a contract SLA — misses are credited back automatically.

04 · Vertical-only

Depth beats breadth.

We turn down work outside our practice areas — and custom-build a new vertical when yours isn't on the shelf. A named human signs off on every SLA-gated workflow.
