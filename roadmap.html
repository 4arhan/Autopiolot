§ 02 — How we pick Three rules, in order.
— Rule 01

Ships an outcome we can bill.

Every roadmap item must end in a completed task a client can pay per-unit for. If it's a feature without a billable outcome, it waits. This keeps our AI automation honest.

— Rule 02

Asked for by paying clients.

Vote counts shown here come from teams already on a contract. We do read inbound requests, but we weight the ask of a team who's actually shipped 10,000 outcomes with us.

— Rule 03

Deepens a vertical, not broadens.

We turn down good ideas if they push us outside our thirteen verticals. A better finance agent beats a mediocre agent for insurance every time.

§ 03 — Open floor

Missing something? Tell us.

If your team would pay for an AI agent we haven't listed, we want to hear it. Every suggestion goes to the engineering lead — not a CRM workflow. We reply inside 5 working days, and if 3 other paying clients want the same thing, it goes straight on the board.

No account required. No newsletter trick. You'll get one email with our reply and, if we build it, one more when it ships.
