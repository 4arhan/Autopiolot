§ 02 — Fine print
Assumptions & methodology

How we arrived at these numbers.

Published rates, published assumptions. No mystery loading, no discount-from-a-number-we-made-up. If anything below doesn't fit your shop, we'll re-price against your actuals during the audit.

Rates are the published per-outcome prices from our Q2 2026 rate card — the same numbers we put on contracts. We tier by workflow complexity: a refund reply is a different cost profile than a lease abstraction, so each workflow carries its own base rate.

Per-vertical overrides account for data volume, compliance surface, and typical rework rates. Healthcare and legal skew higher per-outcome because of audit, PHI, and privilege handling. E-commerce and marketing skew lower because the unit work is shorter and rework is cheap.

Volume discounts kick in on a sliding curve starting at 10,000 / mo and compress to ~40% off list at 500,000 / mo. The slider applies that curve in real time.

An outcome is one unit of finished work, defined per workflow and written into the contract. Examples from production:

  • Patient intake normalised — one complete patient record, structured, de-duplicated, loaded into your EMR.
  • Discovery doc tagged — one document with issue-code tags, privilege flags, and a summary, loaded into Relativity.
  • Reconciliation closed — one bank / ledger pair matched, exceptions flagged, journal entry drafted.
  • Refund resolved — one customer ticket closed with a decision, reply drafted, and the refund executed or declined.
  • CV screened — one candidate scored against a rubric with structured evidence.

If the outcome doesn't pass SLA — wrong tag, missing field, mis-routed — it isn't billable. Credit-back is automatic.

The default A$18 / outcome is a mid-market loaded cost — blended salary (incl. super and payroll tax), tooling, QA, and typical rework. Averaged across the 13 verticals we serve.

Your number will vary. Specialist verticals (paralegal review, senior nurse triage) land closer to A$35–60 per outcome. Low-complexity verticals (shelf-stock refresh, ticket triage) land closer to A$6–10. Override the baseline input with your actual fully-loaded rate for an honest comparison — that's what matters.

We do not assume you fire anyone. We assume the same team ships 3–8x more outcomes, with humans reviewing exceptions and edge cases. The savings are real; the labour dividend is redeployed.

Every workflow ships with an SLA in the contract. Default targets:

  • Outcome plan — 99.1% on-time · credit-back on any miss.
  • Hybrid plan — 99.3% on-time · priority routing · credit-back.
  • Retainer plan — 98.0% on-time · fixed-scope · re-negotiated quarterly.

If we miss the SLA on an outcome, it's not billable. If we miss the monthly aggregate, we credit 2x the shortfall against next month. We publish a live SLA dashboard against your account — you see every miss as it happens.

The calculator shows the recurring monthly bill. One-time and pass-through items sit outside that number:

  • Workflow audit — free. No cost unless we ship.
  • Integration build — billed once at time-and-materials. Typical range A$8K–A$30K depending on source systems.
  • Compliance add-ons — HIPAA / SOC 2 / ISO 27001 evidence packs billed annually (~A$6K / yr).
  • Data egress / archival — pass-through at cloud cost, capped in contract.

We do not pass through token costs, GPU time, or model spend. Infrastructure is our problem.

List price is set in AUD. The USD / GBP toggle applies a fixed conversion for comparison (AUD 1.00 / USD 0.65 / GBP 0.52) — it's a sense-check, not a quote.

We invoice in your local currency at contract-lock rate, re-set annually. If the AUD moves more than 8% during a 12-month term, the contract re-opens for a currency adjustment — same direction, both sides.
