Two programs · side by side

Pick the track that fits
the work you already do.

Referral is for the advisor who drops our name in the boardroom and steps back. Delivery is for the agency that wants to hold the pen in their client's ops console. Different ratecards, different expectations, same philosophy: you earn when outcomes ship.

01· Referral partner

Introduce.
We close.

You make the warm introduction. We run the audit, scope the agents, sign the contract, and deliver. You stay in your lane and collect.

12% of year-1 net revenue, paid quarterly, for 12 months from signed contract.

  • One-page MSA · 48-hour turnaround
  • Joint audit call at your option — not required
  • Quarterly statement · paid in AUD, USD, GBP, or EUR

Section III · How it works 03
Apply → Interview → Onboarding → Launch

Four steps. Twenty-one days.
No surprise paperwork.

Most of our partners are live and paid on their first deal inside three weeks of signing. Here's the whole path, with median durations from our last 18 partner starts.

— Step 01Day 0 – 2

Apply

Fill the form below. We read every submission within two business days. No generic questionnaire — we only ask what we actually need to route you to the right partner manager.

— Step 02Day 3 – 7

Interview

A 45-minute call with our head of partnerships. We walk through your client book, where AI fits, and which track matches your practice. You leave with a written proposal in 48 hours.

— Step 03Day 8 – 18

Onboarding

Sign the one-page MSA. Referral partners get a 90-minute positioning session. Delivery partners begin the three-week certification with two of our senior engineers.

— Step 04Day 19 – 21

Launch

You get a warm-handoff email to two existing clients who fit your vertical, a co-branded deck, and a dedicated Slack channel. First deal typically signs inside 60 days.
