Per-outcome vs. per-token

Most AI vendors bill you per token.
We bill you per finished job.

Token-metered AI punishes you twice: once when the model makes a mistake, and again when you re-run it. Autopilot Labs absorbs that risk. A patient intake costs AU$0.40 — completed, audited, filed. A lease abstract costs AU$6.80 — completed, audited, filed. If the agent fails SLA, it's not on your invoice. That's a commercial guarantee, not a marketing line.

Token / seat pricing
  • You pay for every attempt
  • Retries stack on the bill
  • Hard to forecast monthly spend
  • Vendor wins when the model fails
Autopilot per-outcome
  • One invoice line per job done
  • Retries are on us, not you
  • Forecast = volume × unit rate
  • We only win when the job ships
