Per-outcome vs. per-token

Most AI vendors bill per token. We bill per finished job.

Token-metered AI charges you twice: once when the model makes a mistake, and again when you re-run it. We absorb that risk. A patient intake costs A$0.40 — completed, audited, filed. If the agent misses SLA, it's not on your invoice. That's a commercial guarantee, not a marketing line.

Token / seat pricing

The business case

What this means for your P&L.

Per-outcome pricing turns automation from a fixed software bet into a variable cost that tracks the value you actually receive.

Predictable unit cost

Forecast = volume × rate.

One published rate per outcome, fixed in the contract and re-indexed only at quarterly review. Your monthly spend is simple arithmetic — no token meters, no overage surprises.

Zero idle spend

Quiet month, smaller invoice.

No seats sitting unused, no base fee on the flagship plan, no minimum draw-down. When volume drops, your cost drops with it — automatically.

SLA credit-back

Misses come off the bill.

Every outcome ships against a written SLA. A miss is credited back on the next invoice with no ticket or dispute — and if we miss 2% or more in a month, the whole month is credited.

The fine print

Questions we want you to ask.

The answers every CFO asks for before signing a variable-cost line. If yours isn't here, email us.

What exactly is an "outcome"?
A finished unit of work, defined in the statement of work before we start. For a discovery agent, it's a document tagged and written to Relativity. For a reconciliation agent, it's a ledger entry drafted and approved. Every outcome has a pass/fail SLA criterion — we don't bill the fails.
What happens if an agent misses the SLA?
The outcome doesn't count. It's automatically credited back on your next invoice — no ticket, no dispute. Our SLA also has a cumulative clause: if we miss 2% or more in a calendar month, the whole month's run is credited back, not just the misses.
Do you pass on inference or model costs?
No. Token cost is our problem, not yours. If models get cheaper, we keep the margin — and lower the published rate card at the next quarterly review. We publish the rate-card change history so you can hold us to it.
What are the minimum term, exit terms and minimum volume?
30-day rolling after the first 90 days — no notice period, no exit fee. On churn we hand over agent weights, logs and export artefacts within 30 days, then purge our side; that's in the contract. Minimum volume for per-outcome pricing is 100 outcomes a month — below that the engineering cost doesn't clear, and we'll tell you so during the audit.
Is the audit really free? And what currency do you invoice in?
The audit is free whether you hire us, hire someone else, or do nothing — capped at 18 per quarter, first-come, first-served. Default pricing is AUD; we publish USD and GBP rate cards (converted monthly at OANDA mid) and invoice in your base currency so you don't eat FX volatility. Gulf engagements are priced in AED or SAR.
Can you build a bespoke agent — and who owns the IP?
Inside our thirteen verticals, usually yes: bespoke agents carry a one-time build fee of A$18k–A$48k plus the same per-outcome rate card. Outside them, we'll politely decline. You own the agent weights trained on your data, the logs, and everything the agent produces; we own the base framework. On exit, your agent runs on your infrastructure without us.
